FAQs

Learn what you need to know about Page Speed Solution optimization and what impact it can have to accelerate your success.

What performance optimization tools are used to measure my website speed?

At Page Speed Solution, we use dynamic website optimization tools like Google Pagespeed Insights, GTmetrix, WebPageTest, Chrome DevTools, Search Console, and Pingdom.

What are the factors that impact the website speed?

There are several factors that impact the website speed. Some of them are server configuration, number and size of files that need to be loaded, content caching, code complexity, and third-party services application.

What issues can arise if I do not optimize my website speed?

Slow website loadings can frustrate visitors and they will leave before seeing what you offer. It will lose your traffic and harm your brand reputation. In addition, your website ranking may go low in search results because of a slow site. You will miss out on the valuable traffic and potential customers.
