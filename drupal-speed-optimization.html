How can I check the current speed of my website?

You can easily check the current speed of your website by using various free tools like Google PageSpeed Insights, Pingdom, or GTmetrix. They will analyze your website speed and the performance of your Drupal website.

What are the reasons for the slowdown of my Drupal website?

There can be several reasons for the reduced page loading speed of your Drupal website. Some of the common issues can be excessive or poorly written code, unoptimized images, lack of caching, heavy modules, or slow server response time. However, you need technical expertise and the right consultation from experienced page speed optimization service experts to resolve these issues.

Does site speed affect the SEO ranking of my Drupal site?

With the high site speed, Google will consider your website healthier and eligible for ranking. Generally, faster websites rank higher than others in search results. This is the only way to increase visibility and traffic.
